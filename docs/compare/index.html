Odyz vs Polarsteps, Been, Visited and Apple Photos

Odyz is a free travel tracker for iPhone and iPad that builds your trips, map and digital passport from the photos already on your phone. Here's how it compares with the other ways to keep track of where you've been.

Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Which travel tracker app should I use?

It depends on how you want your travels to get onto the map. If you want it to happen on its own, from the photos you already took, Odyz is the better choice: it fills in your trips, countries and cities automatically, going back years, with no account. If you want to record your route live while you travel, share it with family and print a book afterwards, use Polarsteps. If you'd rather tick countries off a list yourself, or you're on Android, Been and Visited do that well. And if you only want to see your photos on a map, Apple Photos already does it for free.

What sets Odyz apart

  • Your past trips are already in it: Odyz reads the location and date in the photos on your phone
  • No check-ins and no ticking countries off a list
  • No account or email address; your travel history is processed on your device
  • A digital passport of continents, countries and cities, with share of the world explored, days traveling and distance traveled
  • A 3D city map with your photos pinned where you took them
  • A collectible landmark stamp for every place you visit
  • Free, with no ads; Odyz Pro is optional

Odyz or Polarsteps: which is better?

They solve different problems. Polarsteps is a travel journal for the trip you're on: it tracks your route by GPS as you go, lets family follow along, and can turn a trip into a printed Travel Book. Odyz is for every trip you've already taken: it reads your photo library and builds your whole travel history at once, without an account. Many travelers could use both.

Choose Odyz if you want

  • Years of past trips filled in from your photos
  • No account, with your history processed on your device
  • Country and city counts, a passport and stamps

Choose Polarsteps if you want

  • Your route tracked live while you travel
  • Friends and family following your trip
  • A printed book of the trip, or an Android app

Odyz or Been and Visited: which is better?

Been and Visited are checklists: you tap the countries, regions and cities you've been to, and they color in the map. That's quick if you already know your list and you're happy keeping it up to date. Odyz builds the list for you from your photos, so you don't have to remember which year you went where, and it adds the trips themselves: a timeline, a 3D map with your photos, and stats like days traveling and distance. If you're on Android, or you want to plan where to go next with a wishlist, Been or Visited is the better fit.

Odyz or Apple Photos and Apple Maps: do I need an app?

Apple Photos already shows your photos on a map, and in iOS 26 Apple Maps can keep a private list of Visited Places from the day you turn it on, where it's available. If that's all you want, you don't need another app. Odyz turns the same photos into travel history: it groups them into trips, counts your continents, countries and cities, keeps a timeline by year, and adds a digital passport, a 3D city map and a stamp for every place.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to track the countries I've visited from my photos?

If you want your countries, cities and trips filled in from the photos already on your iPhone, use Odyz: it reads the location and date in your photos and builds your map, timeline and digital passport automatically, with no account. Polarsteps, Been and Visited don't build your history from your photo library: in Polarsteps you add photos to each trip by hand, and in Been and Visited you mark the places yourself.

Can Odyz add trips from before I installed it?

Yes. Odyz works from the photos already on your phone, so any trip you took photos on, from last month or ten years ago, shows up on your map, timeline and passport. Apps that track your location, like Polarsteps or Apple Maps' Visited Places, only record trips from the day you turn them on.

Do I need an account to use Odyz?

No. Odyz doesn't need an account or an email address, and your travel history is processed on your device. Polarsteps, by comparison, needs an account (Apple, Google or email) because your trips live on its service.

Is Odyz free?

Yes. Odyz is free to download, with no ads, and its core travel tracking stays free: unlimited countries, regions and cities, with no limits on years or historical data. An optional Odyz Pro subscription adds custom themes, home screen widgets and exclusive travel icons.

Is Odyz available on Android?

No. As of October 2026, Odyz is available for iPhone and iPad only. If you use Android, Polarsteps, Been and Visited all have Android apps.

The short version

Use Odyz to see everywhere you've already been, built from your photos with no effort and no account. Keep Polarsteps for logging a trip live, and Been or Visited if you'd rather keep a checklist by hand.

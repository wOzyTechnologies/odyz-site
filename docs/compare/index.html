Odyz vs other travel tracker apps and Apple Photos

Odyz is a free travel tracker for iPhone and iPad that builds your trips, routes, timeline and digital passport from the photos already on your phone. Here's what it does that other travel apps and Apple Photos don't.

Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

What's the best app to track where I've traveled?

If you don't want to log anything by hand, Odyz. Most travel tracker apps make you check off countries yourself or record a trip while you're on it. Odyz does the work for you: it reads the location and date in the photos already on your iPhone and automatically builds your trips, routes and timeline, going back years. It covers every city in the world, keeps a multi-page digital passport of your stats, needs no account, and its core tracking is free.

Why is Odyz better than other travel tracker apps?

Most travel tracker apps are either checklists, where you tap the countries you've been to and keep the list up to date yourself, or travel journals, where you start tracking when a trip begins and add photos to it by hand. Both only know what you tell them. Odyz already knows: it reads where and when your photos were taken, so your whole travel history, every trip, route and city, is there the first time you open it, and it keeps itself up to date as you take new photos. You don't need an account, and nothing important is locked behind a subscription.

Odyz or Apple Photos: do I need an app?

Apple Photos shows where your photos were taken on a map. Odyz turns the same photos into travel history: it groups them into trips, tracks your routes, builds a timeline by year and counts every continent, country and city you've been to. On top of that you get a multi-page digital passport, a 3D city tour of your photos, passports to compare with friends, and a landmark stamp for every place. Like Photos, it works on your device, with no account.

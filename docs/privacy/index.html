Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Odyz (“the App”). Odyz is a travel tracking application for iOS, owned and operated by wOzy Technologies LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any data that may be collected or processed by the App or through our website.

1. Overview

  • Service: Odyz is designed to help users track their trips and travel history.
  • Legal Entity: wOzy Technologies LLC.
  • Contact Information: For any privacy-related inquiries, please use the contact form on our website.

2. Data Collection

1. Personal Information

  • We do not collect any personal information (such as name, email, phone number) from users.

2. Non-Personal/Usage Data

  • We collect anonymized usage data to understand how users interact with the App and to improve user experience.
  • This data does not include any personal identifiers and cannot be traced back to individual users.

3. Sensitive Data

  • We do not collect or store any sensitive data (e.g., health, financial, biometric).
  • Any travel or location-related information is stored solely on your device and is processed locally; we do not have access to it.

4. Children’s Data

  • The App is not specifically targeted toward children.
  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from minors.

5. Third-Party Data

  • We do not receive any user data from third parties.
  • There are no third-party sign-ins or integrations.

3. Data Use and Processing

1. Purpose of Collection

  • We collect anonymized usage data for analytics purposes to understand how our App is being used and to enhance user experience.

2. Legal Bases

  • We do not store personal data. Our processing of anonymized usage data is based on our legitimate interest in improving the App’s functionality and user experience.
  • Any data the user generates while using the App is stored locally on the user’s device, and the user has full control over it.

3. Automated Decision-Making

  • We do not employ any automated decision-making or profiling based on user data.

4. Storage and Security

1. Data Storage

  • We do not store user data on our servers. All travel information is kept on the user’s device.
  • Anonymized analytics data is collected and stored according to standard analytics practices, but this data cannot be linked to individual users.

2. Security Measures

  • Since no personal data is stored on our servers, our primary security measure is ensuring that no personal data leaves the user’s device.
  • We follow standard security protocols to protect any minimal anonymized usage data (if stored).

3. Retention

  • Anonymized analytics data is retained as long as necessary for legitimate business and analytics needs, in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

1. Third-Party Services

  • We do not use third-party analytics providers, payment processors, or advertising networks that collect or store personal data on our behalf.

2. Disclosure Requirements

  • As we do not collect personal data, we have nothing to disclose in case of legal or business transfers.
  • In the event we are required by law to disclose any anonymized usage data, we would comply with such legal obligations, but users cannot be personally identified from this data.

3. Sale of Data

  • We do not sell user data or share it for marketing or any unrelated purposes.

6. Cookies and Tracking

1. Use of Cookies/Tracking Technologies

  • We do not use cookies or web beacons in the App.
  • The only data we track is anonymized usage data for internal improvements.

2. Purpose of Tracking

  • Anonymized usage data helps us understand how users navigate and interact with the App to improve workflows and features.

3. Opt-Out

  • We do not provide a specific opt-out for this usage data, as it is anonymized and cannot be linked to individual users.

7. User Rights and Controls

1. Access and Correction

  • We do not hold any personal information; therefore, we cannot provide access or correction rights for data we do not possess.

2. Deletion Requests

  • All travel data is stored locally on your device, and you may delete it at any time. Deleting the App from your device removes all such data. There is also an in-app option to delete all data.

3. Consent Withdrawal

  • We do not require consent for data we do not collect, nor do we send newsletters or marketing materials. If you ever have concerns, you can stop using the App or delete all data locally.

4. International Users

  • We comply with privacy regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA to the extent applicable. However, since we do not collect personal data, the requirements for international data transfers do not apply.

8. Updates to the Policy

1. Policy Changes

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website and updated in the App.

2. Versioning/Last Updated

  • This Privacy Policy was last updated on the date indicated at the top. Please review it periodically for updates.

9. Contact or Complaints

1. Privacy Contact

  • We do not have a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO). For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact form on our website.

2. Complaint Filing

  • If you have any complaints or concerns regarding your privacy, please reach out to us through our website’s contact form. We will do our best to address your concerns as quickly as possible.

10. Additional Information

  • At present, we have no additional disclosures or special practices beyond the scope of this Privacy Policy.
  • Because we collect and store no personal data, many aspects of typical privacy regulations regarding data access, correction, or portability do not apply.

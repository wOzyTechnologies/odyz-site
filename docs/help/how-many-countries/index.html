How do I know how many countries I've visited?

Odyz › FAQ › How many countries have I visited?

Published October 21, 2026 · Updated October 21, 2026

The short answer

Odyz counts them for you. It finds every country in your photos and shows the total in your digital passport, next to your continents, cities and the share of the world you've explored. Pick a single year to see how many countries you visited that year. Odyz covers all 193 UN countries and many more territories, and it counts trips from before you installed it too.

Step by step

1

Download Odyz and allow access to your photos

Odyz reads the location saved in your photos and works out which country each one was taken in, going back years.

2

Tap the passport icon

On the main screen, tap the passport icon. The number of countries you've visited is right on the first page, next to your continents, cities and the share of the world you've explored. Choose All Time, or a single year.

3

See how you rank

Flip to the Ranking page to see how much of the world you've seen compared with travelers worldwide.

4

Compare with a friend

Put your passport next to a friend's to compare countries and share of the world explored side by side.

Frequently asked questions

How many countries are there in the world?

There are 193 member states of the United Nations, plus two observer states, Vatican City and Palestine. Many travelers also count territories such as Puerto Rico, Greenland or Hong Kong. Odyz tracks all 193 UN countries and many more territories.

Does Odyz count countries I visited before I installed it?

Yes. Odyz counts from the photos already on your phone, so every country you took photos in is included, however long ago.

Can I see how many countries I visited this year?

Yes. Pick a year in your passport to see how many countries you visited that year.

Why isn't a country I visited counted?

Odyz finds countries from the location in your photos, so a country counts once there's a photo with a location taken there. Take at least one photo in every country you visit.

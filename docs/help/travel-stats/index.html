How do I see my travel stats?

Odyz › Help center › How do I see my travel stats?

Published October 21, 2026 · Updated October 21, 2026

The short answer

Open your digital passport in Odyz. It adds up your travels automatically from your photos: continents, countries and cities visited, how much of the world you've explored, trips taken, days traveling, photos taken and distance traveled. Look at all time or a single year, flip through its pages, and compare your passport with a friend's.

Step by step

1

Open your passport

The first page of your passport shows the big numbers: continents, countries and cities visited, trips taken, days traveling, photos taken and distance traveled. Choose All Time for your whole travel history, or a single year to see just that year.

2

Flip through the pages

Your passport has more than one page. One lists every country you've visited, grouped by continent, with how many cities you visited in each. It comes in light and dark mode.

3

Compare with a friend

Put your passport next to a friend's: both of your countries on one world map, with each of your countries, cities, share of the world explored and distance traveled side by side.

Frequently asked questions

What travel stats does Odyz show?

Continents, countries and cities visited, the share of the world you've explored, trips taken, days traveling, photos taken and distance traveled, plus your visited countries listed by continent.

Can I see my travel stats for one year?

Yes. Your passport shows All Time or any single year, so you can see how much you traveled this year or compare years.

Can I compare my travel stats with friends?

Yes. Odyz shows two passports side by side, with both of your countries on one world map.

Do I need to enter my trips to get stats?

No. Every number in your passport comes from the location and date in your photos, so it's ready the first time you open Odyz and keeps itself up to date.

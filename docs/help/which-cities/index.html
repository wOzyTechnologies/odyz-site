How do I see which cities I've visited?

Odyz › Help center › Which cities have I visited?

Published October 21, 2026 · Updated October 21, 2026

The short answer

Your Odyz timeline lists every trip with the cities you visited and photos from each one, and your map puts a pin on every place you've been. Your passport counts your cities, and you can open any trip to see its photos on the map, or take a 3D city tour. Odyz covers every city in the world, and it all builds itself from your photos.

Step by step

1

Check your timeline

Every trip lists the cities you visited, with photos from each one. Open a country to see all of your trips and cities there.

2

Look at your map

Your map puts a pin on every place you've been, so you can zoom into a region and see every town and neighborhood you've visited.

3

Tour a city in 3D

Open a city to see your photos on a 3D map, right where you took them, inside landmarks or out on the street.

4

Count them in your passport

Your passport shows how many cities you've visited, all time or in any year.

Frequently asked questions

Does Odyz track every city?

Yes. Odyz covers every city in the world, in all 193 UN countries and many more territories.

How does Odyz know which city a photo was taken in?

From the location saved in the photo. Odyz reads it on your device and matches it to the city and country.

Can I see the cities I visited in one country?

Yes. Your timeline groups trips by country, with the cities you visited and photos from each.

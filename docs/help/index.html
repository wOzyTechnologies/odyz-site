Odyz help center

How can we help?

Odyz builds your trips, map and digital passport from the photos already on your phone. Here are the questions people ask most, answered step by step.

Questions

  • How do I track my trips?Track your trips automatically with Odyz: it reads the location and date in the photos already on your iPhone and builds your trips, timeline, map and digital passport, going back years. No check-ins and no account.
  • How do I see my travel stats?See your travel stats in your Odyz digital passport: continents, countries and cities visited, share of the world explored, trips, days traveling, photos and distance traveled, for all time or any year.
  • How many countries have I visited?Find out how many countries you've visited with Odyz: it counts every country in your photos automatically and shows the total in your passport, with your share of the world explored. Covers all 193 UN countries and more territories.
  • Which countries have I visited?See which countries you've visited with Odyz: every country is filled in on your World Trips map, and your passport lists them by continent with the cities in each, built automatically from your photos.
  • Which cities have I visited?See every city you've visited with Odyz: your timeline lists the cities on every trip with their photos, the map pins every place, and your passport counts them. Every city in the world, built from your photos.

Still have a question?

Send us a message, report a bug or ask for a feature. A real person on the Odyz team will get back to you.
